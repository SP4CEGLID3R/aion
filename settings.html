Game

It only listens: nothing is sent to the game or changed. Install Npcap first.

Tools that read the game's traffic may be against AION 2's terms of service and can get your account banned. Use it at your own risk.

Your position

The radar follows you by reading the game's minimap from the screen, 10 times a second. Run the game in borderless or windowed mode and open your wings once after logging in.
