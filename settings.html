Cube radar — settings

Markers on the radar are confirmed, currently spawned cubes only. Static spawn points, predicted locations and past locations are never drawn.

Display

Minimap overlay: put the radar window exactly over the game's minimap (top right), then set the range so the two scales match. The game's minimap is north-up, like the radar.

Minimap tracking

Follows you between the server's position reports by reading the game's minimap (top right) from the screen, like a screenshot: nothing touches the game. Run the game in borderless or windowed mode. After logging in, open your wings once; open them again after moving 10 m or more and the minimap's scale is confirmed.

—

While minimap tracking is on, the radar and the guide arrow are hidden from screenshots and recordings, so they can sit over the minimap without disturbing it.

Data source

Passive and receive-only: copies this PC's game traffic, never touches the game or changes packets. Needs Npcap on Windows. The built-in decoder recognises hidden cubes in Verteron (verified) and learns other zones' cubes when you open one with a key. The game only sends cubes within about 100 m of you.

Source coordinate frame

How the source's axes map to east / north / up. Leave as-is for the simulator.

Packet capture

While recording: press Ctrl+Alt+V in game the moment a cube appears, and Ctrl+Alt+C when you collect one. Then run radar-capture analyze on the recording (see the README).
